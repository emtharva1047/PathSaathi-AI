# Localization and Low-Literacy Design

## MVP language plan
- English
- Marathi as the first regional-language demonstrator
- Locale-ready structure for additional languages after target-user validation

Human-review fixed UI strings and key counselling explanations. LLM-generated output must remain grounded in the same evidence and must not silently redefine official qualification or trade names.

## Low-jargon content
- Prefer short sentences and familiar words.
- Explain placement rate, earnings period, cohort, NSQF and qualification with examples.
- Clarify that an average is not a promise of an individual outcome.
- Provide definitions next to unfamiliar terms.

## Low-digital-familiarity interaction
- Guided flow with one main question at a time.
- Large buttons and touch targets.
- Text labels alongside icons.
- Visible progress, back, edit and help options.
- Avoid long forms and dense learner/parent dashboards.
- Keep language selection visible.
- Offer optional read-aloud support where feasible; audio must never be the only access method.

## Validation
- Review Marathi wording with a fluent speaker before release.
- Test with representative users where feasible.
- Record usability observations without collecting unnecessary personal details.
