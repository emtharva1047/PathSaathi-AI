# User Personas and Journeys

## Student exploring options
**Goal:** Discover practical vocational pathways that align with interests and qualifications.
**Concerns:** Limited awareness, uncertain eligibility, cost, travel distance, family expectations and future study options.

Journey: register and read guidance/consent notice; enter education and broad location context; complete interests/skills/work-preference assessment; review eligible, ineligible and unknown-eligibility pathways distinctly; inspect explanations and sources; shortlist options; invite/link parent or guardian; compare options and generate report.

## Parent/guardian
**Goal:** Understand the student's options and discuss practical constraints.
**Concerns:** Fees, duration, location, course credibility, progression and job-market uncertainty.

Journey: accept a student-authorised invitation and consent flow; review shared assessment summary and sourced information; record independent priorities; compare options with the student; discuss trade-offs and unresolved questions.

## Counsellor
**Goal:** Review a student's evidence and support informed decisions.
Journey: access an authorised summary; review assessment factors, eligibility and explanations; identify missing information; add feedback; preserve original student responses and agency.

## Administrator
**Goal:** Maintain reliable career/course reference information.
Journey: add/update a course record; provide official source URL and last-verified date; mark incomplete fields; review stale records.

## Design principles
- Do not frame a recommendation score as a student's worth or fixed aptitude.
- Preserve student agency while making family input visible.
- Display uncertainty and missing eligibility information clearly.
- Never hide course source, freshness or assumptions.
