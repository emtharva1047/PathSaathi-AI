# Project Research — Initial Problem Framing

## Source of problem statement
The project is based on the SIH problem statement title supplied by the project owner: **AI-Enabled Career Counselling and Family Decision-Support Platform for Vocational Education**.

The complete official SIH brief, sponsor constraints, evaluation criteria and any mandated deliverables have not yet been supplied in this repository. This document therefore records an initial product hypothesis, not a claim that all SIH requirements have been verified.

## Initial problem hypothesis
Students exploring vocational education and their families may need help connecting:
- the student's education and eligibility;
- interests, skills and preferred work environments;
- available vocational qualifications and courses;
- practical constraints such as cost, location and duration;
- possible progression into additional training or education.

Information may be distributed across different provider and qualification sources. Families may also have different preferences. PathSaathi AI proposes to make these factors easier to compare in one explainable decision-support workflow.

These statements are hypotheses to validate through interviews, official SIH materials and source research. No prevalence statistics are asserted.

## Initial target cohort
For a manageable first release, investigate students in India transitioning after Class 10 or Class 12 who are considering vocational courses. Confirm the exact cohort against the official SIH brief before freezing scope.

## Proposed MVP boundaries
The MVP will:
1. collect a structured student profile and assessment;
2. distinguish eligible, ineligible and unknown course eligibility;
3. rank options using transparent, configurable preference matching;
4. present vocational course/career facts with source URL and last-verified date;
5. let students and parents record separate priorities and compare options;
6. generate a report with explanations, uncertainty and next steps.

The MVP will not guarantee admission, job placement, salary, or a particular career outcome. It will not replace a qualified counsellor.
